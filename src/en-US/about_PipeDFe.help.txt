TOPIC
    about_PipeDFe

SYNOPSIS
    Introduces PipeDFe and the monthly fiscal document closing workflow.

LONG DESCRIPTION
    PipeDFe is a PowerShell module that organizes existing Brazilian
    electronic fiscal documents (DF-e), creates monthly closing archives,
    and sends them to the accounting team by email.

    Documents are processed per company and reporting period, independently
    of the ERP that produced them. PipeDFe does not issue fiscal documents
    or query or download documents from SEFAZ services.

    The planned version 1.0 scope covers NF-e, NFC-e, NFCom, CT-e, MDF-e, and
    Brazilian national NFS-e. Companies that issue only national NFS-e
    documents can also participate in monthly closing.

COMPANY CONFIGURATION
    New-PipeCompany registers a company and its XML source directories.
    Get-PipeCompany retrieves registrations; Set-PipeCompany updates them.
    Remove-PipeCompany removes the registration of an inactive company.

    XmlPath identifies the primary XML directory. XmlPathNfse can also be
    configured for national NFS-e. The version 1.0 closing workflow does
    not automatically scan the optional XmlPathEntrada directory.

    Set-PipeSmtp configures the mail server, and Test-PipeSmtp validates
    its settings by sending a test message. Use a test account before
    sending fiscal files to the accounting team.

MONTHLY CLOSING
    Invoke-PipeDFe runs the closing workflow. When dates are omitted,
    the previous full calendar month is selected. When CNPJ is omitted,
    all active companies are selected.

    For each company, the module scans configured XML directories, queries
    its SQLite index, and creates a separate ZIP for each document model
    present in the period. HTML and XLSX reports are separate from the
    ZIP archives and are also attached to the closing email.

    The XLSX workbook includes NF-e, NFC-e, NFCom, CT-e, MDF-e, NFS-e,
    and Occurrences worksheets. CFOP summaries use NF-e and NFC-e item
    values instead of assigning an entire invoice value to multiple CFOPs.

    For national NFS-e, period selection uses the invoice generation date.
    The DPS issue date and service tax period (competencia) are distinct
    fields that are preserved in the report.

ARCHIVE ORGANIZATION
    XML files are grouped by document model and period. Additional folders
    named Enviados or Autorizados are not created. When a document has
    associated events, the invoice and its events may be grouped under
    a subdirectory named after its access key.

DELIVERY SAFETY
    Delivery history is persisted in SQLite. A confirmed delivery is not
    automatically repeated for the same period, archive contents, and
    recipient set.

    If an SMTP attempt has an indeterminate outcome, PipeDFe blocks
    automatic resending. Check the recipient mailbox and mail server
    logs before taking manual action.

    SMTP acceptance does not prove final delivery to the recipient inbox.

EXAMPLES
    Import the module and list its public commands:

        Import-Module PipeDFe
        Get-Command -Module PipeDFe

    Preview closing for a single company:

        Invoke-PipeDFe -Cnpj '11222333000181' -WhatIf

    Run closing for a specific period:

        $period = @{
            Cnpj      = '11222333000181'
            StartDate = '01/10/2026'
            EndDate   = '31/10/2026'
        }
        Invoke-PipeDFe @period

    Check the SQLite index health for a company:

        Get-PipeDFeHealth -Cnpj '11222333000181'

    Replace the sample CNPJ with your company's data before running.

LIMITATIONS
    PipeDFe does not replace professional accounting or fiscal review.
    Numbering gaps indicate documents not found in the local index; they
    do not, by themselves, prove issuance or regulatory noncompliance.

    Export NF-e and inbound import NF-e are processed as fiscal XML files.
    Version 1.0 does not separately extract DI, Duimp, customs tariffs,
    or Siscomex information.

    Attachments are not split across multiple email messages. Confirm
    SMTP attachment size limits before production deployment.

TROUBLESHOOTING
    Verify paths and permissions for the account running scheduled tasks.
    Review Invoke-PipeDFe results and company-specific warnings.

    Use Get-PipeDFeHealth to verify SQLite index existence and schema
    compatibility. This diagnostic does not test the SMTP server.

    If delivery status is uncertain, do not change recipients or files
    merely to bypass duplicate protection. Consult the operations guide.
